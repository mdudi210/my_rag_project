Endpoint Protection & Next-Gen Antivirus






























Level Up Your Security Against Modern Threats


Every company needs a robust cybersecurity solution to protect against these ever-evolving threats. WatchGuard EPP unites next-generation antivirus with self-learning, AI-powered agents to block the daily barrage of cyberattacks. It goes beyond signature-based antivirus, using behavioral analytics and real-time Collective Intelligence to stop malware, ransomware, and zero-day threats invisible to traditional solutions.








 






Key Features












AI-powered protection against malware, ransomware, and emerging threats








Advanced disinfection to quickly restore affected devices








Continuous risk monitoring to strengthen security posture












Real-time protection dashboard, graphs, and reporting








Detect and block both known and unknown malware with Collective Intelligence








Anti-ransomware protection and file encryption recovery
















 






 








Protect Every Device Effortlessly


Your business devices are prime cybercriminal targets. WatchGuard EPP uses AI-powered security to protect against malware, ransomware, and emerging threats. Each endpoint agent connects in real time to WatchGuard Collective Intelligence, a vast threat repository continuously enriched by WatchGuard's Zero-Trust Application Service, ensuring faster detection of unknown attacks.










 






 








Centralized Endpoint Management


Manage all your security needs from one intuitive Cloud-based console. Simplify operations and boost security management without requiring specific infrastructure. Enjoy easy deployment with automatic uninstallers for rapid migration and an intuitive web-based interface, making device management a breeze.










 






 








Secure Your Endpoints in Seconds


Activate instant protection across your enterprise with a single lightweight agent without impacting endpoint performance and requires no complex tuning. Get complete coverage for all Windows, macOS, and Linux systems, plus Android and iOS devices, whether online or offline.










 






 








Endpoint Risk Monitoring


Cyber risks can severely impact your systems if endpoint solutions aren't properly configured. Continuously assess and strengthen your security posture with endpoint risk monitoring. Discover vulnerabilities and configuration weaknesses across endpoints where WatchGuard EPP is implemented to prevent infections and disruptions.










 






 








Unmask System Weaknesses


Why start patching when it’s already too late? Identify system and software vulnerabilities early and take proactive steps to fix them. WatchGuard EPP’s vulnerability assessment tool helps you evaluate and prioritize security weaknesses, keeping systems secure and your business ahead of emerging threats.
