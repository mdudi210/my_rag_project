Patch Management & Data Security
































Extend Your Endpoint Protection with Add-On Modules


Boost your endpoint security with WatchGuard add-ons available for select EPP, EDR, EPDR, and Advanced EPDR products. Add Patch Management to centrally manage updates and patches for operating systems and third-party applications. Deploy Full Encryption to encrypt and decrypt endpoint information. Rely on our Advanced Reporting Tool to uncover unusual behaviors. And include Data Control to discover, classify and monitor unstructured personal data stored on endpoints.










 






 








Streamlined Patch Management


Discover, prioritize, and deploy critical Windows, macOS, and Linux operating systems and third-party application patches. Prevent cyberattacks and mitigate known vulnerabilities effortlessly with WatchGuard Patch Management.


Patch It All with WatchGuard










 






 








Full Disk Encryption


WatchGuard Full Encryption strengthens control over hard disks and USB drives containing sensitive data, ensuring that the data remains protected from theft and unauthorized access.


Secure Data with Encryption










 






 








Monitoring & Actionable Insights


WatchGuard Advanced Reporting Tool generates security intelligence in real time by delivering deep insights into your applications, network, and users' day-to-day operations and actions.


Identify and Alert










 






 








Privacy & Data Protection


WatchGuard Data Control monitors personal and sensitive data on endpoints. Automatically discovers and audits personal data to help comply with regulatory requirements.


It is available for use in these countries: Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, and UK.


Protect Your Sensitive Data
