Organizations






























One Cybersecurity Platform Fit for Any Business


Identifying and preventing the latest cyber threats is a modern business imperative. With built-in comprehensive protection, integration, deployment flexibility, and operational efficiency, WatchGuard's Unified Security Platform® architecture is the right choice for any organization, regardless of size or industry.








 










Small Businesses




Small businesses face a unique set of cybersecurity challenges due to resource constraints. Without the necessary staff and budget to dedicate to security, small businesses continue to account for over half of the breaches every year.


Safeguard Your Small Business >








Midsize Businesses




Midsize businesses represent a significant focus for cybercriminals, as they are sizable enough to offer lucrative payoffs, but often lack the sophisticated cybersecurity defenses of larger enterprises. Many midsize companies have no dedicated security personnel and instead rely on already overburdened IT teams for protection.


Master Midsize Business Security >








Distributed Enterprises




With the rise of remote work and hybrid business models, organizations have become more distributed than ever before. In addition to securing the flow of data between traditional corporate headquarters and remote work sites, many distributed enterprises must also support multiple locations that operate like individual small businesses.


Protect Your Distributed Enterprise >








Managed Service Providers




Managed service providers (MSP) provide initial security deployment and configuration offerings, ongoing maintenance, monitoring, reporting, and more. In today’s complex threat landscape, MSPs need a security provider with a platform that elevates security efficacy while streamlining and simplifying every aspect of security delivery.


Explore Keys to Managed Security Success >
