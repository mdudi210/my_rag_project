Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) 
































Transform Your Endpoint Security with WatchGuard




Try Now












Prevent, Detect and Respond to Cyber Threats


Endpoints are prime targets for cybercriminals, who exploit vulnerabilities and outdated software. WatchGuard Endpoint Security delivers robust protection with minimal complexity, removing the guesswork. Our AI-powered solutions, WatchGuard EDR, EPDR, and Advanced EPDR, combine endpoint protection and EDR with the Zero-Trust Application Service to autonomously detect and respond quickly and accurately.








 






Watchguard Endpoint Security: A Critical Element of Our Unified Security Platform


WatchGuard’s Unified Security Platform architecture enables security and IT teams with comprehensive protection for networks, endpoints, Wi-Fi, and identities. The platform features include, at no additional cost, WatchGuard Cloud, which delivers operational automation, centralized administration, visibility, and reporting; ThreatSync, a true XDR solution; and native cross-product capabilities that strengthen security posture.










 






Secure Your Digital Assets










 






 








Unmask and Mitigate Unseen Threats


WatchGuard Endpoint Protection Detection & Response (EPDR) combines EPP technologies, advanced EDR, and self-learning AI-powered agents and services to protect computers, laptops, and servers from threats invisible to traditional solutions.


Make Zero Trust a Reality










 






 








Take Full Control of Advanced Threats


WatchGuard EDR complements other EPP or antivirus solutions by adding a full stack of AI-powered technologies to automate the detection, containment, and response to any advanced threat.


Elevate Your Endpoint Defenses










 






 








Maximizing SOC Efficiency


WatchGuard Advanced EPDR extends WatchGuard EPDR beyond security management to security operations with intelligent capabilities for rapid investigation and response.


Stop Threats in Their Tracks
