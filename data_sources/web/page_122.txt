WatchGuard Wins 2024 ChannelVision Visionary Spotlight Award






17 July 2024
 
 By 
The Editor










Share on LinkedIn












Share on Facebook












Share on X










Share on Reddit














 






We’re excited to share that ChannelVision Magazine has recognized WatchGuard Technologies with a 
2024 Visionary Spotlight Award
 for Cybersecurity in the program’s Business Technology category! The annual award program honors top industry players that have significantly advanced their channel and technological investments over the last year. 


Specifically, the Visionary Spotlight Awards highlight channel and service provider innovation in communications, honoring products, services, deployments and channel contributions across categories that span Overall Excellence, Business Technology and Service Provider Technology. Editors from Beka Business Media, as well as a panel of judges from independent industry resources, assess hundreds of applications. Criteria included overall innovation, future industry impact, creativity, feature set differentiation, ease of use and interoperability.




 






The judging panel found WatchGuard to demonstrate numerous opportunities for channel partners to boost their roles as trusted partners for business success. “This is our largest Visionary Spotlight Awards to date,” 
said Berge Kaprelian
, founder of Beka Business Media and publisher of ChannelVision. “We were thoroughly impressed with the candidates’ worthiness, commitment to innovation and strides throughout the channel.”


Read more about ChannelVision’s Visionary Spotlight Awards program and view the complete list of 2024 winners 
here.
 Stay tuned to our 
blog
 for all the latest WatchGuard announcements, insights, and industry honors.
