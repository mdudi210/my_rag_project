1. Choose Your Product
















 








Firewall Security


Award-winning tabletop, rackmount, virtual, and cloud-based Firebox appliances deliver the industry’s most sophisticated level of protection against today’s threats.












 








Identity Security


AuthPoint not only reduces attacks arising from weak or stolen credentials, but it’s delivered entirely from the Cloud for easy setup and management.












 








Secure Wi-Fi

Endpoint Security


Advanced cybersecurity solutions integrating EPP, EDR and machine-learning technologies combined with DNS-level protection to achieve full endpoint protection.














2. Select Security Package










Firewall Security Packaged to Your Needs


WatchGuard offers three packages of security services on our Firebox T and M Series appliances, as well as our Firebox Cloud and FireboxV virtual models:




Along with stateful firewalling, the 
Standard Support
 license includes centralized management, 24x7 support, full VPN capabilities, and built-in SD-WAN.


The 
Basic Security Suite
 includes all the traditional network security services typical of a UTM appliance: IPS, antivirus, URL filtering, application control, spam blocking, and reputation lookup. It also includes our centralized management and network visibility capabilities, as well as our standard 24/7 support.


The 
Total Security Suite
 includes all services offered with the Basic Security Suite, plus AI-powered malware protection, ThreatSync (XDR), enhanced network visibility, endpoint protection, cloud sandboxing, DNS filtering, and the ability to take action against threats right from WatchGuard Cloud, our centralized management hub.

Endpoint Security Purchase Options


The WatchGuard Endpoint Security portfolio provides a wide range of integrated, Cloud-based products to protect endpoints and remotely manage and control systems.
