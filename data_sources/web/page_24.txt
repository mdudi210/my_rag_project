Whether you’re securing a small office, a distributed enterprise, or cloud infrastructure, our next-generation firewall (NGFW) lineup includes tabletop and rackmount appliances, virtual FireboxV solutions, and Firebox Cloud for seamless network protection.

Get the Best Firewall for Your Small Business


WatchGuard’s T Series tabletop Fireboxes feature the latest Wi-Fi and high-speed ports to reduce bottlenecks and enhance app performance. From entry-level to top-tier, each firewall model provides comprehensive security services, enabling growing businesses to expand securely, whether in small offices, branch locations, or retail environments.


Compare Enterprise-Grade Firebox Hardware










 






 








Scale Up with Firebox Rackmount Appliances


Purpose-built for high-demand environments like distributed enterprises and data centers, the midrange rackmount Fireboxes (M Series) deliver up to 5x faster VPN and 3x UTM throughput. The expanded series provides more deployment options and greater flexibility, allowing businesses to choose the best fit for their network environment.


Compare Top-Rated Rackmount Systems










 






 








Top of the Line Performance & Security


Top of the line, with modular port options that allow businesses to create their ideal M4800 or M5800 deployment, and dual-redundant power supplies for highest performance and uptime. These appliances are ideal for the central headquarters location of a large distributed enterprise organization, offering optimal flexibility to evolve the appliance over time.


Firebox M4800 & M5800










 






 








Protect Any Environment with Virtual Firewalls


Unleash the power of virtualization without security sacrifices. FireboxV delivers top-tier network security built specifically for virtual environments. Now, you can enjoy the benefits of virtualization with the peace of mind of knowing your virtual machines are as secure as your physical ones.


Elevate Your Hybrid Network Security










 






 








Add Firebox Cloud for Full Security


Extend trusted network security to public cloud environments with Firebox Cloud. Get the same robust unified threat protection used in on-premises deployments, ensuring consistent, enterprise-grade defense for virtual servers and critical virtual workloads.


Complete Your Stack with Cloud Firewalls










 




 






Extend the Reach of Your Security










WatchGuard’s Unified Security Platform® architecture simplifies administration by unifying network, identity, and endpoint security into a single management system. Firebox firewalls serve as the foundation, extending protection across the network. Integrated capabilities like multi-factor authentication (MFA), ThreatSync (XDR), and managed detection and response (MDR), strengthen defenses against credential-based attacks and advanced threats, delivering comprehensive security for modern businesses.


Get One Platform for End-to-End Protection
