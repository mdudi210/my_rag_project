Benefits for the MSP






























Everything MSPs Need


WatchGuard’s managed service providers (MSPs) are equipped with a flexible program, a powerful and diverse product portfolio, and an ecosystem of technology integrations that enable you to leap ahead of the pack in an increasingly competitive and growing marketplace.  Here’s some of what you can expect from WatchGuard:












✓




FlexPay
 - Dynamic term, subscription, and points-based payment models.








✓




Pause and Play
 - Quickly enable or disable security services for customers.








✓




Common MSP Tools
 - Integrate with  MSP tools like RMMs and PSAs.












✓




Custom, Automated Reports
 - Make your value visible with custom, automated reports.








✓




Expert Support
 - Leverage extensive trainings and around-the-clock technical support.








✓




Bundle Services
 - User-focused security services bundled for ease of deployment and profitability.
















 






Key Benefits for the MSP










 






 








Dynamic Payment Models for Partner Success


Bridge the disconnect between vendor and customer payments with WatchGuard FlexPay. With three versatile payment options ‒ term contracts, subscriptions, and points ‒ that can be mixed to best suit your needs, FlexPay is a key factor in maximizing the profitability of every customer account.


Learn More about Flexible Payment Options










 




 




Excellence in the Security Channel


WatchGuard has been named a 2025 Canalys Cybersecurity Champion for fueling MSP growth.


Get the Report










 






 








Centralized, Cloud-Based Management


Manage all your clients’ security in the multi-tier, multi-tenant WatchGuard Cloud platform.  We focus on automation and reducing the number of actions you need to take to keep your security ahead of the latest threats.


Smart Security Delivery Begins with an Automation Core










 






 








Designed for MSPs


AuthPoint user inheritance allows you to reduce operating costs and create flexibility for you and your team when it comes to managed customer accounts. Give more control to your staff without exposing your business to a cyberattack or investing in additional MFA licenses.


Learn About AuthPoint User Inheritance










 






 








Zero-Touch Deployment


Avoid having network administrators on site to configure customer devices.  Our MFA and endpoint security services are automatically installed from WatchGuard Cloud, and for security hardware, simply ship new devices to remote locations and plug in to download templated configurations.
