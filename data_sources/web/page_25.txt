Firebox Protection Packages for Every Level of Security


Every WatchGuard Firebox includes future-ready firewall security services and 24x7 reliable support to provide comprehensive protection against intrusions, phishing, malware, ransomware, and other cyber threats. Find the firewall protection package that fits the level of security you need to stay ahead of cybercriminals and protect your entire network.
















 














What is a firewall?











      A firewall is a network security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predetermined security rules. Firewalls can be hardware-based, software-based, or a combination of both, and they play an important role in protecting network environments from malicious activities. 
    














What is a firewall security service?











      Firewall security services are essential for cybersecurity, offering robust protection against threats, vulnerabilities, and data breaches. With built-in technologies like packet filtering and stateful inspection, firewalls are intelligent gatekeepers that protect trusted networks and ensure secure communication across digital environments.
    














Why do I need network security services?











      Firewall security services offer protection beyond blocking traffic. They detect threats continuously, safeguard data, simplify compliance, and provide visibility into cyberattacks that basic firewalls miss. Upgrade to advanced firewalls for better security and peace of mind.
    














What are the key firewall security services?











      Core network security services include intrusion prevention service (IPS), advanced malware detection, DNS filtering, and secure VPN access. These essential cybersecurity solutions protect your business against evolving cyber threats, ensuring safe, compliant, and reliable network operations.
    














What are the benefits of firewall security services?











      Network services provide layered defense against modern cyberattacks, reduce IT management complexity, ensure compliance, and extend the value of your firewall investment with integrated, always-on protection.
    














Which firewall package is right for me?











      Choose the right WatchGuard suite based on your needs: Basic Security Suite fits growing teams and compliance requirements, while Total Security Suite delivers advanced protection for enterprises, critical infrastructure, and expanding networks.
