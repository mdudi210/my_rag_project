WatchGuard Cloud for Partners






























Cut Through the Chaos


WatchGuard Cloud Makes Security Simple and Effective


WatchGuard Cloud eliminates the cost and complexity typically required to manage, log, and report on all security products. There's no need for any physical or virtual infrastructure, no maintenance costs, no downtime, and no jumping back and forth between platforms. It's a simple solution to advance your business, help you deliver your security solutions more effectively, and free up time to focus on the value-added work that matters most to your customers.








 






 








Streamline Operations and Reduce Costs


Simplify your security operations and save time with WatchGuard Cloud. Manage all your clients from one central location, eliminating the need for multiple logins and boosting efficiency. Automate repetitive tasks, freeing your team to focus on strategic growth. Plus, WatchGuard's flexible payment options, like subscriptions and points, let you tailor your investment to your business needs. Focus on what matters most – delivering top-notch security.










 






 








Enhance Network Visibility and Control


 WatchGuard Cloud provides unmatched visibility and control, giving you a bird's-eye view of all your clients’ security postures. Identify and respond to threats faster with a unified dashboard showcasing all your clients' security in one place. Stay ahead of the curve with access to real-time threat intelligence, and understand your client’s security needs with customizable reports, allowing you to track key metrics and trends for proactive protection and informed decision-making.










 






 








Increase Efficiency and Productivity


 Simplify your workflow and boost your team's output with WatchGuard Cloud. Generate custom security reports automatically, freeing valuable time for other crucial tasks. Pre-configured security templates let you quickly deploy solutions to new clients, minimizing setup time. Plus, you can manage everything remotely, offering the ultimate flexibility and responsiveness to your clients' needs.










 






 








Enjoy Sustainable Business Growth


Utilize WatchGuard Cloud to drive revenue growth and profitability. You benefit from scalability, allowing seamless client base expansion without the hassle of managing complex infrastructure. With simplified service deployment, easily introduce new revenue streams like Wi-Fi to your existing clients. Enhance client retention by delivering superior security outcomes supported by centralized management and advanced threat protection, reducing incidents and increasing satisfaction. 










 






 








Make Compliance Simpler


Impress your clients with built-in reports and increase your capabilities with a WatchGuard Compliance Reporting license. Generate HIPAA, PCI DSS, and other reports effortlessly with automated features, saving you valuable time. Plus, reduce security risks and potential compliance violations, giving you peace of mind and strengthening client trust.


Experience Automated Continuous Compliance










 






 








Rely on ISO 27001 Certification


WatchGuard has obtained ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification of its information security management system (ISMS). The scope of the certification is limited to the ISMS of the WatchGuard infrastructure for the configuration, management, support, and delivery of the products listed on our certificate.
