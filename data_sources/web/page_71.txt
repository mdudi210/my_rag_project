Partner Specializations & Certifications






























Get Rewarded for Your Achievements


WatchGuardONE certifications are individual achievements available to your staff, which contribute to your organization's overall WatchGuardONE specialization in each of our product families. The greater the number of specializations that your business earns and maintains, the greater your partner status and financial rewards.








 






 








WatchGuardONE Sales Certifications


Sales certifications are offered as “learn on your own” online programs with dedicated learning paths for each product family. Complete the learning path for any product family, and you’ll earn a WatchGuardONE sales certification – an important part of your overall product specialization.










 






 








WatchGuardONE Technical Certifications


WatchGuard offers partners the opportunity to take technical certification exams, which also count toward your overall product specialization. The certification demonstrates to your customers that you are selling products with which your employees have a high level of technical competence. Certification exams are offered through third-party test proctor Kryterion, and individuals who pass these exams are certified for two years.










 




WatchGuard offers technical certification exams for the following product families:




Network Security


Secure Wi-Fi


Multi-Factor Authentication


Endpoint Security




See Detailed Exam Options in the Technical Certifications FAQ >
