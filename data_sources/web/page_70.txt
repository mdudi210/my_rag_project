Efficiency for MSPs


WatchGuard’s Unified Security Platform is perfect for MSPs because it simplifies client management while enabling rapid, efficient, and profitable growth. It streamlines service delivery by bringing the management of normally disparate security tools into a single Cloud console. This unlocks efficiencies simply not possible with disconnected systems and allows you to automate frequent manual tasks so that you can spend more time delivering value to customers.


Simplify Managed Security with WatchGuard Cloud










 






 








User-Focused Security for MSPs


More accurate user identification is the key to future cybersecurity success.  Our risk framework policies improve identity management capabilities with customizable and flexible rules to configure users and devices based on level of risk. Furthermore, MSPs can easily manage authentication policies across multiple client deployments to prevent external threats and thwart potential data leaks from within. Our framework easily scales to support client growth and portfolio adoption.


Support Client and Portfolio Adoption










 






 








AI-Enabled XDR for MSPs


As WatchGuard’s built-in XDR solution, ThreatSync Core correlates, prioritizes, and enables automated or manual remediated across the platform to increase MSPs’ security efficacy and productivity. ThreatSync+, WatchGuard’s Open XDR solution, leverages a unique AI engine to deliver advanced detections and rapid response throughout the WatchGuard stack and beyond for open networks and Cloud systems, and more.


Discover and Remediate More Threats










 






 








Managed Services for MSPs


The platform includes WatchGuard MDR, a managed security service that makes it possible for our MSP partners to address the rising demand for 24/7 managed detection and response capabilities. Overseen by an elite team of AI-enabled cyber experts, the solution helps you elevate your offerings with ongoing monitoring, threat hunting, detection, containment, and guided remediation services from WatchGuard you can seamlessly extend to your customers.


Mature Your Service Offerings with MDR
