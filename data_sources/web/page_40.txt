Cloud Management 






































WatchGuard Cloud


Go Beyond Security Management










See the bigger picture in WatchGuard Cloud.




Try It Now














 






Enhance your security management using WatchGuard Cloud, ensuring smooth administration and consistent security policies. Access a complete overview of your network activity with proactive threat monitoring and immediate access to vital security data. Demonstrate your dedication to robust network security through customizable and impactful reports, fostering trust and confidence.










 






Platform-Wide Clarity and Control


WatchGuard Cloud is the centralized management hub for our Unified Security Platform, offering streamlined visibility and control over all connected WatchGuard security solutions. This simplifies management and enhances efficiency, allowing for a cohesive and intelligent security posture.










 






 








Reimagine Network Management


See it all, manage from anywhere. WatchGuard Cloud delivers comprehensive visibility, configuration control, and real-time threat monitoring, empowering you to manage your network security from anywhere with an Internet connection. This simplifies your security management, saves time, and allows you to make informed decisions confidently.


Take Control of Your Network Security










 






 








Automate Detection and Response (XDR)


WatchGuard ThreatSync uses cutting-edge AI technology to automate and simplify continuous monitoring, detection, and remediation of threats across multiple IT landscapes. With a cloud-native and open architecture, you get affordable, easy-to-operate solutions delivering the latest innovation.


Improve IT Security Workloads With XDR










 






 








Streamline Identity Security Deployments


Managing Identity Security & MFA in WatchGuard Cloud allows easy configuration, user synchronization, and policy enforcement – all delivered through a secure, cloud-based infrastructure. This simplifies your deployments, streamlines user experience, and strengthens your overall security posture.


See Who’s Who and Secure What They Do










 






 








Unlock the Secrets of Secure Wi-Fi


Effortlessly manage and secure your WatchGuard Wi-Fi with WatchGuard Cloud. Enjoy centralized configuration, real-time monitoring, and powerful guest access and reporting tools – all from one cloud platform. Streamline your Wi-Fi management for enhanced security and performance.


Boost Wi-Fi Security and Performance










 






 








Ease Endpoint Security Management


Unify, monitor, and respond – all from one place. WatchGuard Cloud becomes your central hub for comprehensive endpoint security, simplifying management and boosting effectiveness. Secure your network, endpoints, and users with ease.


Control and Defend Your Devices










 






 








Secure Remote Work with Zero Trust Simplicity


With more employees working remotely, organizations need a simple way to deliver consistent protection everywhere. FireCloud Total Access makes zero trust access easy. Managed through WatchGuard Cloud, security policies follow users seamlessly across remote, cloud, and on-premises environments.


Protect Your Remote Workforce










 






 








Streamline On-Premises Management


If direct control and data privacy are top priorities for your organization, look no further than our robust on-premises management solution. This includes WatchGuard System Manager (WSM) and Dimension, empowering you with granular configuration and the ability to manage your WatchGuard appliances, even offline.
