Firebox Rackmount Appliances




























Powerful Performance for Evolving Networks


Engineered for modern business needs, our Firebox M Series rackmount appliances provide next-generation firewall (NGFW) features with up to 5x faster single-tunnel VPN performance and 2-3x higher UTM throughput. Simplified licensing across services gives businesses full-stack security without hidden costs or complexity. These powerful devices are perfect for securing branch offices, distributed networks, and large data centers.

What Are the WatchGuard Firebox Advantages?


Growing organizations can’t afford complexity, downtime, or runaway costs. WatchGuard’s next-gen Firebox rackmount appliances deliver enterprise-grade performance and reliability without the premium price tag.










 






 








Get Real-World Price-Performance


Firebox rackmount appliances deliver over twice the performance gains in UTM, HTTPS, and BOVPN, tested with all services active. No need to disable security to meet benchmarks, ensuring consistent protection and throughput for demanding environments like branch offices and data centers.










 






 








Run Security Services on a Unified Platform


Integrated with WatchGuard Cloud and ThreatSync, Firebox rackmounts offer centralized management, AI-powered XDR correlation, and empowering security integrations. This unified approach reduces complexity, speeds up response times, and strengthens security across endpoints, identities, and networks, all from a single platform.


Elevate Your Security Posture










 






 








Rely on Transparent Licensing


With multi-speed ports, scalable memory, and redundancy options included, Firebox rackmounts eliminate hidden costs. Predictable licensing helps MSPs protect margins, simplify pricing for customers, and deliver enterprise-grade security without surprise fees, ideal for growing businesses and distributed deployments.










 






 








Protect More with Trusted Security Services


WatchGuard’s rackmount Firebox models offer more than hardware; they provide powerful security services like advanced threat detection, endpoint protection, identity security, and centralized management, all integrated into WatchGuard’s Security Suites.
