Endpoint Security Prime






























The New Baseline for Endpoint Protection


Next-generation antivirus (NGAV) alone is no longer enough to protect your organization. WatchGuard Endpoint Security Prime is an EDR-first, AI-powered offering that combines NGAV and full attack surface reduction (ASR) controls in one simple, affordable package. Prime sets a new standard for endpoint protection, delivering enterprise-grade security without enterprise cost or complexity.


Available in North America Only.








 






 








Go Beyond Antivirus


Modern threats, such as fileless and living-off-the-land attacks, easily bypass prevention-only antivirus. Behavioral detection and automated response stop these threats in real time before they spread.










 






 








Advanced Protection, Accessible Price


Proper endpoint security should be affordable for all organizations. AI-powered detection, automated response, and continuous monitoring deliver stronger protection at a cost that is affordable for small and midsize organizations.










 








Simplify Endpoint Security


The WatchGuard Endpoint Security Prime console handles prevention, detection, and response – no extra tools or complexity. Instantly see what’s happening across every device and act instantly when something looks suspicious.




Gain full visibility across endpoints


Review incidents in context


Prioritize and reduce exposure


Identify your most critical vulnerabilities














 






 








Dynamically Reduce the Attack Surface


The best defense is a good offense. Built-in controls, including vulnerability assessment, discovery of unmanaged endpoints, firewall, device control, and web filtering tools, close the gaps that attackers exploit, making your organization a harder target.










 






 








Smarter Detection with Adaptive AI


Human-supervised Adaptive AI learns across executables, installers, scripts, and other formats to stop threats pre-execution. On-device models decide in real time, even offline, resulting in fewer tickets for MSPs and fewer disruptions for customers.
