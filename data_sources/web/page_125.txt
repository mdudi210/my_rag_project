WatchGuard Wins Three 2024 Global InfoSec Awards






20 May 2024
 
 By 
The Editor 










Share on LinkedIn












Share on Facebook












Share on X










Share on Reddit














 






We’re delighted to share that Cyber Defense Magazine has honored WatchGuard Technologies with three awards in its 2024 
Global InfoSec Awards program
! The coveted annual awards, now in their 12th year, recognize information security (InfoSec) innovators from around the globe who deliver a unique and compelling value proposition for their product or service. 




 






A panel of independent judges selected the winners in each category. Selection criteria included how nominees demonstrate an understanding of tomorrow's threats today, provision of cost-effective solutions, and innovation in new ways to help mitigate cyber risks and stay ahead of the next breach. 


Here is the full lineup of WatchGuard’s 2024 Cyber Defense Magazine Global InfoSec Award wins: 




Next Gen, Extended Detection and Response (XDR): 
WatchGuard ThreatSync XDR
 


Best Service, Managed Detection and Response (MDR): 
WatchGuard MDR
 


Best Solution, Zero Trust: 
WatchGuard Zero-Trust Application Service
 




Read more about Cyber Defense Magazine’s Global InfoSec Awards program and view the complete list of 2024 winners 
here
. Stay tuned to our 
blog
 for all the latest WatchGuard announcements, insights, and industry honors.
