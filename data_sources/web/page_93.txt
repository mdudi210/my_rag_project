Certification




























WatchGuard offers customers and partners the opportunity to take technical certification exams. These exams are offered at 
Kryterion testing centers
 worldwide, or through their online proctoring service. If you’re interested in certification, we recommend you review the frequently-asked questions list below.










What technical certifications does WatchGuard offer?




 








WatchGuard offers these technical certification exams. All exams are available in English, French, German, and Latin American Spanish.




Network Security Essentials for Locally-Managed Fireboxes
  – This exam tests your knowledge of basic networking and how to configure, manage, and monitor a locally-managed WatchGuard Firebox. 


Network Security Essentials for Cloud-Managed Fireboxes
  – This exam tests your knowledge of basic networking and how to configure, manage, and monitor a cloud-managed WatchGuard Firebox. 


Identity Security Essentials (formally named Multi-Factor Authentication Essentials)
  – This exam covers basic authentication technology concepts and how to set up, configure, and manage WatchGuard AuthPoint.


Secure Wi-Fi Essentials with WatchGuard Cloud
  – This exam covers general wireless concepts, and and how to manage WatchGuard access points in WatchGuard Cloud.


WatchGuard Endpoint Security Essentials
  – This exam covers basic WatchGuard Endpoint Security with Patch Management, Full Encryption, and the Advanced Reporting Tool.
















How do I create an exam account?




 








You do not need to create a separate account for technical exams. From the 
Technical Certification Exams
 page in the Learning Center, click the "Exam Account" icon and your exam account will be automatically created through single sign-on. 














Where can I take a technical certification exam?




 








WatchGuard offers technical certification exams at authorized 
Kryterion testing centers
 worldwide. If it is not convenient for you to travel to a testing center, you can also use Kryterion's online proctoring service to take an exam from your home or office.














How do I register to take a technical certification exam?




 








You can register by following these steps:





Step 1
  – Log in to the WatchGuard Learning Center. 


Step 2
  – On your Dashboard, click Technical Certification Exams. 


Step 3
  – Click Exam Account to automatically log in to your WatchGuard Webassessor exam account through single sign-on.
















I have a Webassessor account that is not associated with my WatchGuard account. Can I still access this account?




 








No, you will not be able to access an existing WatchGuard branded Webassessor account that is not associated with your WatchGuard account.














How much does it cost to take an exam?




 








All exams are US $200 and can be paid with any major credit card. You pay Kryterion for the exam when you register to take the exam. The price is the same whether you go to a Kryterion testing center or take the exam through Kryterion's online proctoring system. Each test taker must create their own account and the exam must be registered and paid for while logged in as that test taker.















Does the cost of the exam include tax?




 








By law, Kryterion is required to collect appropriate tax in many jurisdictions around the world. This tax will be calculated based on the location of the scheduled exam session at time of purchase and will be added to the total exam cost. If your company is tax exempt, please email 
Tax@kryteriononline
 to confirm, mark your account exempt, and process refunds.














How do I prepare for an exam?




 








Hands-on experience is the best way to prepare. We also provide a Study Guide (login required) for each exam. These guides can be found in the 
WatchGuard Learning Center
 and they supplement the basic recorded video training available. They also include a description of the exam, information on what to study to prepare for the exam, and several sample exam questions.














Does WatchGuard offer practice exams?




 








No. We do include sample questions in the exam section of the Study Guide we provide for each exam. These sample questions help you understand what kind of questions you can expect to see on the exam. The exam section of the Study Guide also includes important information on the content that we test on and we recommend you review it closely. These guides can be found in the 
WatchGuard Learning Center
 (login required).

Note that there are some online sites that offer WatchGuard practice exams but these sites do not work with WatchGuard and are not reliable.














How and when can I launch my exam?




 








The 
Launch
 button appears 10 minutes before the exam is scheduled to start and remains visible for 20 minutes, or until you click it to begin. For more details see the Kryterion provided guide: 
How to Launch an Online Proctored exam
.














Are there special requirements to take an online proctored exam?




 








Yes, and we recommend that you allow at least 24 hours between the time you schedule an online proctored exam and when you take the exam to make sure you have met all requirements. You can find detailed information on these requirements in the 
Kryterion Test Taker Guide
. To summarize:




Install the secure browser software, which will lock your computer down during the exam to prohibit the use of computer test aids.


Create a biometric profile based on facial recognition. Make sure no other people show in the background of your photo.


You can use either an internal laptop webcam 
OR
 an external webcam for your online proctored exams.


Verify your computer OS and installed applications support the online proctoring process.


Make sure your desk area is clear and your mobile phone is not accessible. You may be required to remove and show all sides of watches, bracelets, or any large jewelry, to make sure they are not technology-enabled. For more information, see the Kryterion Guide: 
How to Prepare for an Online Exam Pre-check
.


During the exam, you cannot eat, speak out loud, or interact with other people.
















How can I contact Kryterion Support if I’m having technical issues during my online exam?




 








You can contact Kryterion Support directly by creating a case with the Kryterion Candidate Community Case Form 
 HERE
. This form includes a “Live Chat” option.














What do I need to bring with me when I take a certification exam at a Kryterion testing center?




 








You must bring two (2) forms of personal identification (ID) that match the name used to register for the exam. Both forms of ID must have your signature, and one of the two forms of ID must have your photo. Valid forms of identification include a current driver's license, current passport, or equivalent. Food, laptops, backpacks, notepads, pens, or other personal equipment are NOT allowed in the test area. 
Please note that during the exam check-in process, you will be asked to turn out exterior pockets (on jackets, jeans, slacks, etc.) to verify that the pockets are either empty or that they do not contain any prohibited items. You may be required to remove and show all sides of watches, bracelets, or any large jewelry to make sure they are not technology-enabled.














Can I use reference materials during a certification exam?




 








No reference material or study aids are allowed when you take the exam.














What score is required to pass a WatchGuard certification exam?




 








You must achieve a score of at least 75% to pass.














When will I get my exam results?




 








You will receive your exam results appear on the screen immediately. You will also see high level feedback in a section-by-section percentage score format. If you pass the exam, you receive an email that includes a PDF certificate and a link to download an email signature you can use to promote your certification. If you are a WatchGuard Partner and pass the exam, it can take several days for your technical certification to appear in the Partner Portal.














If I fail the exam, can I retake it?




 








If you fail an exam, you can take it again at the one-time retake cost of US $100. Subsequent attempts are US $200. To request a coupon code for the one-time 50% retake discount, email 
[email protected]
. There is no waiting period between failing an exam and trying again.














Can I get a detailed report of my exam answers?




 








To maintain the integrity of the exam, we do not provide detailed information on your exam answers. High-level feedback is provided at the end of your exam in a section-by-section percentage score format.














How long will my certification be valid?




 








Certification is valid for one year. 














Can I get my exam purchase receipt?




 








When you purchase an exam, you get an automatic confirmation email as your receipt. To generate an invoice, from your exam account, select 
Generate Receipts
. If your PDF receipt does not automatically generate, make sure automatic downloads are enabled for your browser. See the 
Generate Exam Receipt instructions
 for more details.














What do I need to do if I have to cancel or reschedule my exam appointment?




 








You can reschedule or cancel your exam from your exam account 
My Exams
 tab. The policy to reschedule or cancel an exam is different depending on the exam delivery type.




Online Proctored Exams:  If you cancel or reschedule your exam within 24 hours of the scheduled exam time, Kryterion will charge a fee of $55 USD.


Onsite Proctored Exams at Kryterion Test Centers: If you cancel or reschedule your exam within 72 hours of the scheduled exam time, Kryterion will charge a fee of $85 USD.




If you miss your scheduled exam appointment, you forfeit your exam payment and are not eligible for a refund.














What is the refund policy?




 








Refunds are granted by our exam delivery partner, Kryterion, when you cancel your exam with 72 hours notice for onsite exams and with 24 hours notice for online exams. No other refund requests are granted.














Are special accommodations available for people with disabilities?




 








WatchGuard will provide disability accommodations on request. 
Contact us
 for further information.














Will my exam activity be available in the Learning Center?




 








Yes! The Learning Center will now include a list of any technical certification exams that you pass in the Completed tab of your Learning Dashboard. The certificate will not be available in the Learning Center, but we will continue to send the certificate via email when you pass an exam.


The Learning Center will not include information related to scheduled exams, failed exams, exam scores, or exam expiration dates.
