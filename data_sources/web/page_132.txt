NOTICE TO CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS


This Notice to California Residents (“Notice”) explains how we collect, use, disclose, sell, share and retain personal information about California residents and the rights afforded to them under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by California Privacy Rights Act (further “CCPA”). It is intended solely for, and is applicable only as to, California residents.


WatchGuard acts as a service provider for most of the personal information it receives or processes through the Services and follows the instructions of its customers that engaged WatchGuard with respect to how it processes your personal information. If you would like more information about how your personal information is processed by our customers, please contact those companies directly.


Notice at Collection of Personal Information


We currently collect, and in twelve (12) months prior to the Last Update Date of this Notice have collected, the following categories of personal information:




Identifiers:
 such as name, email address, login details, account number, mailing address, phone number, details about your computers, devices, applications, and networks, including internet protocol (IP) address, cookie IDs and other online identifiers that are automatically assigned to your computer or device when you access the Internet.


Internet or other electronic network activity information:
 such as your browsing history, search history, information regarding your interactions with internet websites, applications, and advertisements, our sites and Services (e.g., products or services you looked at or searched for online and the Services you used), details about your internet app, or network usage, including URLs or domain names of websites you visit, information about the applications installed on your device, or traffic data, pages that you visit before and after using the Services, the date and time of your visit, the amount of time you spend on each page, the links you click and pages you view within the Services, how you interact with the pages and forms and the difficulties you may be having with them, or other actions you take through use of the Services such as your preferences. This may also include our Services performance information, fault reports , crash logs, authentication logs, session data and aggregate or statistical information.


Geolocation data:
 such as your IP address derived approximate physical location.


Commercial information:
 including your purchases and engagement with our sites and Services, your contact preferences for marketing communications, data you provide when you contact WatchGuard for customer or technical support, which may include data about the state or condition of your device, or the content of your chats and other communications with WatchGuard.


Financial information:
 including your credit card numbers and associated financial identifiers and billing address but only where you pay us directly for the Services.


Audio and visual information: 
such as call recording data that may be used to verify your requests and enable WatchGuard to provide support services and investigate security incidents. We may also photograph or record WatchGuard events you participate in and publish such content on our website and social media accounts.


Professional and employment-related information:
 such as your employer’s name, address, email and phone number, your job title and department, as well as information you provide when you apply for a job with us, such as your resume, desired pay, education and work history, and visa status. When you apply for a job with us you may also choose to provide your veteran status, disability status, and links to your website, blog, portfolio, or LinkedIn profile.


Inferences:
 such as information gathered from your preferences or interests. We derive this information from the other types of personal information we collect, including internet or other electronic network activity information and commercial information.




We collect this personal information directly from you your devices or third-party sources as described in the 
Information You Provide to Us
,

, and 
Information We Collect from Other Sources
 sections of our Privacy Policy. Not all categories of personal information are collected from each source.


In addition to the purposes detailed in the 
Why We Collect and How We Use Your Information
 section of our Privacy Policy, we currently collect and have collected the above categories of personal information for the following business or commercial purposes:




Auditing related to counting ad impressions to unique visitors, verifying positioning and quality of ad impressions, and auditing compliance with this specification and other standards.


Helping to ensure security and integrity of our Services to the extent the use of your personal information is reasonably necessary and proportionate for these purposes.


Debugging to identify and repair errors that impair existing intended functionality of our Services.


Short-term, transient use, including, but not limited to, non-personalized advertising shown as part of your current interaction with us, provided that your personal information is not disclosed to another third party and is not used to build a profile about you or otherwise alter your experience outside the current interaction with us.


Performing our Services, including maintaining or servicing your accounts with us, providing customer service, processing or fulfilling orders and transactions, verifying customer information, processing payments, or providing similar services.


Providing advertising and marketing services, except for cross-context behavioral advertising, to you provided that, for the purpose of advertising and marketing, our service providers and/or contractors shall not combine the personal Information of opted-out consumers that the service provider or contractor receives from us, or on our behalf with personal information that the service provider or contractor receives from, or on behalf of, another person or persons or collects from its own interaction with you.


Undertaking internal research for technological development and demonstration.


Undertaking activities to verify or maintain the quality or safety of our Services and devices that are owned, manufactured, manufactured for, or controlled by us, and to improve, upgrade, or enhance our Services and devices that are owned, manufactured, manufactured for, or controlled by us.


Advancing our commercial or economic interests, such as by inducing another person to buy, subscribe to, or provide our Services, or enabling or effecting, directly or indirectly, a commercial transaction.

Information We “Share” and “Sell”


Under the CCPA, “sale” means the disclosure of your personal information by us to a third party in exchange for money or any other thing of value. We do not sell your personal information in the conventional sense (i.e., for money), but engage in online activities that may constitute a sale of personal information under the CCPA.


“Sharing” under the CCPA means our disclosure of your personal information to a third party in order to serve you behavioral or interest-based targeted advertising. Please note that CCPA’s definition of “sharing” does not cover disclosure of your personal information to third parties for our “business purposes”, such as improving and securing our Services or providing customer support.

California Residents Privacy Rights and How to Exercise Them


If you are a California resident, you have certain rights with respect to your personal information outlined below. We encourage you to exercise your rights and will not discriminate against you for doing so. You can submit privacy rights requests by emailing us at 
[email protected]
 or by calling us at 1-877-232-3531, unless instructed otherwise below.


Opt Out of “Sale” or “Sharing”.
 You have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of your personal information, as defined by the CCPA. To learn how CCPA defines “sales” and “sharing” of personal information, please refer to 
Information We “Share” and “Sell”
 section above. If you would like to opt out of CCPA “sales” and “shares” of your personal information that was collected through cookies and similar technologies used on our website, please opt out through our 
Cookie Preference Center
. You may also opt out through user-enabled privacy controls, such as a browser plugin, privacy setting or other mechanism, that communicate or signal your choice to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. If you have enabled privacy controls on your browser (such as a plugin), we will also treat that as a valid request to opt out. Please see 
Opt-Out Preference Signals
 section of our Privacy Policy for more information.


After opting out, you may continue to see non-targeted advertisements on our Services. You may also continue to see advertising that could be based on personal information processed before you opted out. Please note that opt out action needs to be repeated from each browser you use to access our webpages. You will also need to repeat the process if at any point you clear the cookies from a particular browser.
