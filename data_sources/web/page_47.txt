Quarterly Internet Security Report


Our quarterly security reports are based on anonymized data from active WatchGuard products and services worldwide, internal and partner threat intelligence, and a research honeynet. Review the findings each quarter for the latest on emerging cybersecurity trends, challenges, and best practices from the WatchGuard Threat Lab team.

Threat Landscape Tracker


Evolving malware and network attacks continue to redefine the threat landscape. Your security posture is only as strong as your understanding of the latest attack types, techniques, and trends. Our Threat Landscape Tracker offers the up-to-date malware and network attack data you need to safeguard your business, partners, and customers against cyberattacks.


Get Real-Time Security Insights










 






 








Ransomware Tracker


Access to in-depth ransomware intelligence can give you the tools required to defend against cyber extortion. Leverage our Ransomware Tracker to examine the latest data on the prevalence of ransomware attacks, their origins, active or inactive status, and more.

About the WatchGuard Threat Lab


WatchGuard’s Threat Lab is a group of threat researchers committed to discovering and studying the latest malware and Internet attacks. The Threat Lab team uses data from WatchGuard’s Firebox Feed, internal and partner threat intelligence, and a research honeynet, to provide analysis and practical security advice about the top threats on the Internet.
