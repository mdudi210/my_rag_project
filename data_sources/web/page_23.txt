Network Security






































Unified Security for Hybrid Enterprises










See the Big Picture with WatchGuard Cloud




Try It Now














 






WatchGuard’s network security solutions are designed for modern IT needs and are perfect for businesses maintaining on-premises environments, transitioning to the cloud, or operating in a hybrid setup. Our comprehensive network security stack ensures end-to-end protection with award-winning firewalls, NDR, Zero Trust Network Access, secure Wi-Fi, and advanced services. It’s built-in protection against today’s top threats, so your business remains uninterrupted.










 






 








Activate Next-Level Security


Streamline your security strategy with WatchGuard Fireboxes. Designed for the modern IT landscape, our award-winning Firebox appliances provide reliable protection for on-premises networks, while FireboxV secures virtual environments effortlessly. Extend your security posture to the cloud with Firebox Cloud, safeguarding your data wherever it lives. Firebox is known for industry-leading threat detection, simplified management, and scalable performance that ensures business continuity across all environments.


Upgrade Your Network Defense










 






 








Access Without Limits


Extend your network to anywhere your users work.  FireCloud enables remote workers to securely connect across hybrid on-premises and cloud environments through Zero Trust Network Access, Firewall as a Service (FWaaS), VPN, and Secure Web Gateway (SWG) protections. FireCloud protection makes remote access as seamless and safe as being on-site.


Protect Remote Workers










 






 








Power Up with Firewall Security Services


Don't settle for plain network security; elevate it with powerful options from WatchGuard. Our Basic Security Suite shields against common threats and the Total Security Suite adds advanced defenses with AI and sandboxing. Both packages offer centralized control and 24/7 support – focus on your business, and we'll handle your security.


Get Advanced Protection for Your Network










 






 








Unmask Hidden Threats


ThreatSync+ NDR, a cloud-based solution, leverages advanced AI to analyze your network traffic, identify suspicious activity, and issue real-time alerts. It empowers you to investigate and respond to potential attacks quickly, preventing them from causing damage. With threat correlation, detailed reports, and automated threat detection, you can gain valuable insights into your network health and proactively address vulnerabilities.


Stay Ahead of Evolving Threats










 




 






Unified Security for Enhanced Defense










WatchGuard's Unified Security Platform architecture expands beyond network security by providing comprehensive protection for endpoints, identities, Wi-Fi, and more, with cross-product threat intelligence, centralized management, and operational agility. Take a unified approach to blocking increasingly sophisticated and evolving threats.


Enable Unified Protection Today










 






 








Keep Your Business Connected


Spotty Wi-Fi and unsecured connections have you concerned? Secure Wi-Fi delivers the perfect blend of performance and security. Our cloud-managed access points blanket your office with reliable, high-speed Wi-Fi for all your devices. Advanced security features, like WPA3 encryption and network segmentation, keep your business data safe. With centralized management, easy guest access options, and seamless integration with WatchGuard's security ecosystem, you can ensure a smooth and secure Wi-Fi experience for everyone.


Boost Performance and Enhance Security










 






 








Manage Smarter, Not Harder


Simplify your network security management with WatchGuard Cloud. This centralized platform puts control at your fingertips – configure firewalls, access points, and security services from anywhere. Gain real-time network visibility with detailed reports and automated alerts. Plus, WatchGuard Cloud scales with your business, allowing you to manage multiple locations and devices easily.
