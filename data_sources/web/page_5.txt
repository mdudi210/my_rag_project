SD-WAN






























SD-WAN


SD-WAN is a game-changing, software-based routing solution that streamlines network management and optimizes performance. It automatically distributes network traffic across multiple WAN connections based on policies you define. Leverage SD-WAN to improve network reliability, scalability, agility, cost efficiency, and even your security posture.














 














What is SD-WAN? 











      SD-WAN is a powerful routing solution that distributes traffic between networks or to a specific network based on firewall policies geared toward performance and cost optimization.  
    














How does SD-WAN work?











      SD-WAN technology delivers a virtual overlay that centralizes WAN connection management and intelligently distributes traffic based on established policies around performance requirements, bandwidth usage, and more.
    














What are the benefits of SD-WAN? 











      SD-WAN can offer performance improvements, cost savings, streamlined deployments, flexible configuration, and better protection for distributed users and workloads.  
    














What is SD-WAN vs. VPN?











      SD-WAN solutions help organizations connect data, applications, and users across many disparate locations with tight control, deep visibility, and high performance. VPNs send traffic from remote devices and locations through a dedicated network link for safe connections to the corporate network. 
    














 






Prioritizing Secure SD-WAN










 




Security is a critical factor in SD-WAN deployments, yet many products don’t specifically require advanced security integration. Some network security providers that offer SD-WAN solutions deliver essentially the same functionality as dedicated SD-WAN providers but with crucial security capabilities built in.
