Managed Security Services for MSPs






































Managed
Services


Ease Security
Operations










As threats evolve, devices multiply, networks become more complex, and cybersecurity skills shortages climb to record highs, many organizations turn to managed security services (MSS) providers to help shore up internal security gaps and access enterprise-level security at an accessible cost.








 






Streamline Managed Security Services with WatchGuard










 






 








Managed Detection and Response (MDR)


A team of seasoned security experts works as an extension of your team, at a fraction of the cost of building out a SOC and in-house staﬀ. WatchGuard MDR monitors, detects, investigates, and contains threats for you across your customers’ IT environment around the clock.


Team Up with WatchGuard >










 






 








Attack Surface and Risk Reduction


Prevent cybercriminals from breaching your customers’ unprotected endpoints, compromised credentials, vulnerable and end-of-life software, and sprawling networks. Monitor and protect every threat vector with WatchGuard's Uniﬁed Security Platform architecture.


Deliver Powerful Proactive Services >










 






 








Vulnerability and Patch Management


Both are vital to securing customers and keeping all operating systems and software up to date. Ransomware relies on unpatched vulnerabilities to spread. This is where WatchGuard Patch Management can help you become more eﬀective and eﬃcient.


Reduce Your Customers' Attack Surface >










 






 








Security Compliance


Maintaining compliance is becoming increasingly diﬃcult for customers, as the regulations around security tighten. MSPs have a unique opportunity to help their customers comply with the regulations that aﬀect them, including PCI DSS, GLBA, SOX, HIPAA, FISMA, and more.


Assist Businesses in Complying with Regulations >










 






 








Cyber Insurance


Understanding and meeting cyber insurance requirements can be complicated. To avoid confusion, companies rely on MSPs to advise them on the right insurance coverage and provide guidance on complying with insurers’ requirements when applying for a policy.


Help Customers with Cyber Insurance >










 




 






 A Unified Approach to Managed Services










Cyber threats will never stop evolving, and cybersecurity will grow in sophistication. Your managed security services play a pivotal role in keeping your customers secure, and you need a uniﬁed, simpliﬁed approach to security.


You Need the Security of ONE Platform >
